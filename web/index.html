        Scam types ScamShield recognises

        These are the patterns it looks for most. Each one lists what it usually looks like and what to do. It also recognises lottery and prize, instant loan, investment and fake bank alert scams.

        Fake KYC or account block

        Looks like: A message says your account, card or SIM will be blocked unless you update KYC through a link.

        Do this: Banks do not do this by SMS. Open your bank's official app or visit a branch.

        Requests for your OTP or PIN

        Looks like: Someone asks you to read out or forward a one-time password, PIN or CVV.

        Do this: Never share these, even with someone who says they are from your bank.

        Fake refund or UPI request

        Looks like: You are told to approve a request or enter your UPI PIN in order to receive money.

        Do this: You never need a PIN to receive money. Decline the request.

        Parcel and delivery fees

        Looks like: A parcel is said to be held, and a small fee or address update is requested through a link.

        Do this: Check orders only in the official courier or shopping app.

        Job and task offers

        Looks like: Easy daily earnings for liking videos or doing tasks, often with a fee to join.

        Do this: Real employers do not charge you to hire you. Do not pay or share documents.

        Electricity or connection cut-off

        Looks like: A threat that power will be cut tonight unless you call a number or pay at once.

        Do this: Check your bill in the official app or on the electricity board's website.

        Police or official impersonation

        Looks like: A message or caller says a case is registered against you and keeps you on a video call, called "digital arrest".

        Do this: Hang up. Officials do not arrest anyone or demand money over a message or call.

        Remote-access apps

        Looks like: You are asked to install an app such as AnyDesk, or an APK file, to "fix" a problem.

        Do this: Never install it. If you already did, uninstall it and call your bank on the number on your card.
