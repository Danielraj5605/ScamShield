1. What ScamShield is

Names of organisations that appear in example messages belong to their owners and are used only to describe scams that imitate them.

2. It gives advice, not guarantees

  • ScamShield estimates how likely a message is to be a scam. It can be wrong in both directions: it can rate a genuine message as risky, and it can miss a real scam.
  • A result of "no common warning signs found" does not mean a message is safe.
  • The result is not legal, financial, security or professional advice. Do not rely on it alone to decide whether to send money, share a code or click a link.
  • If you are unsure, contact the organisation using the number printed on your card or in its official app, not a number in the message.
  • If you have lost money to fraud in India, contact your bank straight away and call 1930 or report at cybercrime.gov.in.
