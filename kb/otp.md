# OTP, PIN and card-detail theft
types: otp_theft, fake_alert
A one-time password (OTP) approves a payment or a login. Anyone who asks you to read out, forward or type your OTP, UPI PIN, ATM PIN, CVV or password is trying to take money from your account, even if they say they are from your bank, from customer care, or are "cancelling a wrong transaction". Banks never ask for these codes. Real OTP messages always say "do not share this code".
