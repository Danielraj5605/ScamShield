# Fake KYC and account-block messages
types: kyc, fake_alert
Scammers send messages saying your bank account, card, SIM or wallet will be blocked unless you "update KYC" or link PAN or Aadhaar through a link. Banks and telecom companies do not ask customers to complete KYC through links in SMS or WhatsApp. The fake page copies the bank's look and steals your login details, OTP or card number. The safe action is to ignore the link and check your account only in the bank's official app, on the official website typed by hand, or at a branch.
