# Remote-access apps and APK files
types: remote_access
Apps like AnyDesk, TeamViewer and QuickSupport, or an APK file sent over WhatsApp, can let a stranger see your screen, read your OTPs and control your phone. No bank or utility will ask you to install such apps. If you installed one at someone's request, uninstall it, change your banking passwords, and call your bank using the official number.
