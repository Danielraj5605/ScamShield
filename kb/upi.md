# Fake refunds and UPI collect requests
types: refund_upi
In UPI, you only enter your PIN to send money, never to receive it. Scammers send "collect requests" disguised as refunds, cashback or a payment "sent by mistake", and ask you to approve the request or enter your PIN. Approving it takes money out of your account. Decline unknown requests. If someone says they paid you by mistake, check your bank balance yourself, and let your bank handle any genuine reversal.
