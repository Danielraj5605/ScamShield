# Police and official impersonation ("digital arrest")
types: impersonation
Fraudsters pretend to be police, CBI, customs, the narcotics bureau, TRAI or court officials. They say a parcel with drugs or a money-laundering case is linked to your Aadhaar, and keep you on a video call, calling it "digital arrest", until you transfer money for "verification". There is no such thing as a digital arrest. Real officials do not arrest people or demand money over phone or video calls. Disconnect and report it.
