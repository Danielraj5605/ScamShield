# What to do if you clicked or paid
types: kyc, otp_theft, refund_upi, parcel, job, utility, impersonation, prize, loan, investment, remote_access, fake_alert
Act fast: call your bank on the number printed on your card to block the card or account, and report the fraud on the National Cyber Crime Helpline 1930 or at cybercrime.gov.in. Reporting quickly gives the best chance of stopping the money. Keep screenshots of the messages, links and phone numbers.
